# Presentation Outline

## 1. The healthcare problem
Diabetes care is often retrospective. The opportunity is to move from seeing a high glucose value to anticipating a high-risk state.

## 2. The Digital Twin
A continuously refreshed virtual patient combining EHR history with wearable/CGM signals.

## 3. Why this outcome?
A 2-hour glucose-spike forecast is specific, measurable and demonstrable in a short PoC.

## 4. Data fusion
Show the static EHR layer beside the dynamic sensor stream and then the combined feature vector.

## 5. Model
Compare an interpretable Logistic Regression baseline with a Random Forest model.

## 6. Demo
Select a virtual patient, show the glucose trajectory, risk probability, risk tier and current state variables.

## 7. Real-world workflow
Wearables/EHR -> Digital Twin -> risk flag -> clinician review -> human decision.

## 8. Scale-up
Future work: real CGM integration, personalized baselines, calibration, prospective validation, clinician feedback and privacy-preserving deployment.

## 9. Safety
The PoC flags risk only. It does not diagnose or prescribe.
